## English

**Name (30):** Tiktako

**Subtitle (30):** Football trivia games

**Promotional text (170):**
Test your football knowledge: noughts and crosses with footballers, starting XIs of classic matches, a 5-player quiz and more. Play a friend or online opponents.

**Description:**
Watching football is one thing. Knowing it is another. Tiktako turns football knowledge into short, fast, competitive games.

HAT-TRICK
Noughts and crosses with footballers. Name a player who fits the club, country or trophy in the row and column to take the square. First to 3 games wins the series. Play a friend on one phone, online with a code, or a random opponent. Pick a league: Süper Lig, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga.

MISSING 11
Fill in the starting XI of a classic match. A new match every day, unlimited practice, or pick your team. Derbies, European nights, international finals.

FOOTBALL QUIZ
Online quiz for up to 5 players. 10 questions, 15 seconds, 4 options. Fast and right wins. Level up and climb the weekly standings.

MYSTERY PLAYER
Name the footballer from their clubs, country and trophies. Every wrong guess opens another clue.

FOOTBALL BINGO and POSSESSION PLAY
Fill the card with the fewest footballers, or steal your rival's ground on a hex pitch.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone, and you can delete all your data from Settings at any time.

## İnceleme notu (App Review Information → Notes)

> No login is required. Single-player and same-phone games work offline. Online modes (Hat-trick "With a friend" and "Random opponent", Football Quiz, Possession Play online) connect to our game server over HTTPS. When no other player is searching, random-opponent modes pair the player with a computer opponent so a game can always start. Nicknames shown to other players are filtered for offensive words on the server, and Settings > Delete my data removes everything from the phone and the server. There are no purchases, ads, analytics or tracking in this version.
>
> To try an online room alone: open Football Quiz > Random opponent; a game starts within 15 seconds.
