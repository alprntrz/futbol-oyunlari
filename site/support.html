Support

Found a problem or have an idea? Get in touch.

Contact: Open a message (needs a GitHub account; messages are public, so don't include personal details).

Common questions

Online games won't connect. Check your internet connection and try again. If it keeps happening, let us know.

A player's details are wrong. Tell us the player's name and the game you saw it in, and we'll fix it.

How do I delete my data? In the app, Settings → Delete my data. Details in the privacy policy.

No sound. Make sure your phone isn't on silent and Settings → Sound effects is on.
